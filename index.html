05 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Still wondering whether this would make sense for your team? That's what the free audit is for.

Ask us anything ↗
Do I have to change the tools we already use?+

Usually not. We'll first look at the systems you have and what their built-in features can already do. If something is missing, we can recommend a targeted connection or workflow.

Will this replace someone on my team?+

That's not the goal. We focus on repetitive tasks like acknowledgments, follow-up reminders, and lead routing. Your team keeps control of important conversations, pricing, and decisions.

What kind of businesses do you work with?+

We're focused on businesses with appointment or estimate-based sales processes, especially HVAC, plumbing, roofing, and other home-service teams. The best fit is a company with consistent inquiries or open estimates to follow up on.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing depends on the workflow, your current systems, and support needs. We'll identify the opportunity first and outline a clear scope, implementation cost, and any monthly fees before you commit.

Can you guarantee more bookings?+

No one can responsibly guarantee that. We can help you build a more consistent process and measure what changes. The goal is to improve response and follow-up, then evaluate actual outcomes together.
